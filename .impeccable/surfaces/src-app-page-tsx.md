## Direction contract

THESIS: The fleet as the river's own almanac — 79 vessels reckoned on one graduated measure and published as a table of record. Refuses the aggregator arrangement where a photo grid leads and the score is a badge; here the audited number leads and photography corroborates.

OWN-WORLD: Laid almanac paper ground (#F4EFE4), ink #1C1A15, lapis #1B3A5C as the sole action accent, oxidized bronze #8A6A3B as structure only, reed green #5E7A6B for verified states. Letterpress ruled tables, hairline-to-heavy rule hierarchy, a graduated nilometer scale as the recurring device. No cards; records are ruled rows. Numerals at cubit scale set the hierarchy.

STORY: The visitor understands that every vessel was audited by the platform, not the operator; believes the five-dimension score is evidence rather than marketing; and either filters the measure to their own floor or posts an anonymous request against it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: No hero photograph. Full-width graduated measure across the top, five bands as named stations, 79 vessel ticks distributed along it. Beneath, the search instrument as one ruled line of inline fields (dates, guests, quality floor) with the primary action at its right terminus. Below the fold line, the fleet begins as ruled records — score numeral left at cubit scale, vessel name and route centre, price right.

FORM: The Flood Almanac, candidate 7 of the grounded list, seed key f8181599.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
